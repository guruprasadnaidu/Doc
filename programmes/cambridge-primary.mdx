The Cambridge Primary programme at Harvest International School is where your child's lifelong love of learning begins. Designed for students in Year 1 through Year 6 (ages 7–12), this programme builds a strong academic foundation across core subjects while nurturing the curiosity, creativity, and confidence your child needs to thrive — not just in school, but in the world beyond it.

## Programme Overview

Cambridge Primary spans six years, from Year 1 to Year 6, welcoming students aged 7 to 12. During these formative years, your child develops essential skills in literacy, numeracy, and scientific thinking, all within a nurturing and stimulating environment. Small class sizes ensure that every student receives the individual attention they deserve, and our teachers build genuine relationships with each child to support both their academic and personal growth.

## Subjects Offered

Your child will study a broad and balanced curriculum that covers both core academic disciplines and creative development:

- **English** — Reading, writing, speaking, and listening skills developed progressively across all year groups.
- **Mathematics** — Number sense, problem-solving, and mathematical reasoning built from the ground up.
- **Malay** — Proficiency in Bahasa Malaysia in line with Ministry of Education Malaysia requirements.
- **Science** — Hands-on, inquiry-driven exploration of the natural and physical world.
- **Information & Communication Technology** — Foundational digital skills and responsible technology use.
- **Visual Arts** — Creative expression through drawing, painting, and mixed media.
- **Music** — Appreciation, performance, and the joy of musical discovery.
- **Physical Education** — Active learning, teamwork, and healthy habits for life.

## Assessment

Your child's progress is monitored continuously throughout the year using a balanced mix of formative and summative assessments. Formative assessment — including class discussions, observation, and short tasks — helps teachers adapt their teaching in real time so your child never falls behind unnoticed. Summative assessments at the end of terms give a clear picture of achievement against Cambridge standards.

At the end of Year 6, students have the opportunity to sit the **Cambridge Primary Checkpoint** assessments in English, Mathematics, and Science. These externally marked tests provide an internationally benchmarked report of your child's performance, giving you a clear, credible picture of where they stand as they prepare to transition into Cambridge Secondary.

## Digital Citizenship

In today's connected world, knowing *how* to use technology is as important as knowing *how to* use it responsibly. Our integrated Digital Citizenship programme teaches your child to engage with technology across five key principles:

- **Safe** — Protecting personal information and staying safe online.
- **Appropriate** — Using digital tools in ways that are suitable for the context.
- **On-task** — Staying focused and using technology purposefully.
- **Respectful** — Treating others with kindness and respect in digital spaces.
- **Honest** — Being truthful and ethical in all digital interactions.

These values are reinforced across subjects and year groups, ensuring your child develops into a thoughtful and responsible digital citizen.
