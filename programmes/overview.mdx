## The Cambridge Pathway

At Harvest, your child's education is a single, connected journey. Our Cambridge Primary and Cambridge Secondary programmes are designed to build on each other seamlessly, so your child never has to start over — they grow. In the Primary years, students develop curiosity, foundational skills, and a love of learning. As they move into Secondary, those foundations are deepened into rigorous academic disciplines and internationally benchmarked qualifications.

Skills are developed progressively across every year group. Critical thinking introduced in Year 1 becomes structured analysis in Year 9. The creativity nurtured through Primary Arts becomes entrepreneurial thinking explored in Business Studies. This continuity means your child arrives at every stage of their education well-prepared, confident, and ready to excel.

## Why Cambridge?

The Cambridge International curriculum is one of the most respected educational frameworks in the world. Here is why we chose it as the foundation of every programme at Harvest:

- **Internationally recognised qualifications** — Cambridge credentials are accepted and valued in over 160 countries.
- **Recognised by the world's leading universities** — From institutions in the UK and US to Australia, Singapore, and beyond, Cambridge qualifications open doors.
- **Develops critical thinking and creativity** — The curriculum goes beyond rote memorisation, encouraging students to analyse, evaluate, and create.
- **Backed by a not-for-profit curriculum board** — Cambridge Assessment International Education reinvests in research and educational quality, not shareholder returns.
- **Licensed by the Ministry of Education Malaysia** — Harvest International School is fully licensed, giving you confidence and peace of mind.
