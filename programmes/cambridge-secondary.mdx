The Cambridge Secondary IGCSE programme at Harvest International School is where your child transforms their foundational skills into world-class qualifications. Designed for students in Year 7 through Year 11 (ages 13–17), this rigorous programme challenges students academically, develops their independence as learners, and prepares them for entry into universities and higher education institutions around the globe.

## Programme Overview

Cambridge Secondary spans five years, from Year 7 to Year 11, for students aged 13 to 17. Over these years, your child moves through Cambridge Lower Secondary (Years 7–9) and into the internationally recognised Cambridge IGCSE (Years 10–11). The curriculum deepens in academic rigour with each passing year, guided by experienced teachers who maintain the small class sizes and personalised attention that define the Harvest experience.

## Subjects Offered

Your child will study a broad, academically demanding curriculum that prepares them for the full range of university pathways:

- **English** — Advanced reading, writing, and communication skills for academic and professional contexts.
- **Mathematics** — Comprehensive mathematical study encompassing algebra, geometry, statistics, and more.
- **Additional Mathematics** — Extended mathematical study for students pursuing STEM pathways.
- **Malay** — Continued development of Bahasa Malaysia proficiency.
- **History** — Critical analysis of historical events, sources, and their global significance.
- **Information & Communication Technology** — Advanced digital skills, systems thinking, and responsible technology use.
- **Business Studies** — Foundational understanding of business, enterprise, and the global economy.
- **Economics** — Analysis of economic systems, markets, and real-world financial decision-making.
- **Biology** — In-depth study of living systems, from cells to ecosystems.
- **Chemistry** — Exploration of matter, reactions, and the chemical principles that shape our world.
- **Physics** — Investigation of forces, energy, waves, and the fundamental laws of the universe.

## IGCSE Qualifications

The Cambridge IGCSE is one of the most widely respected secondary school qualifications in the world. Taken at the end of Year 11, these externally assessed examinations are marked by Cambridge Assessment International Education and produce results that are recognised by universities, employers, and governments in over 160 countries.

Your child's IGCSE results provide an internationally benchmarked record of their academic achievement — a credential that carries genuine weight when applying to universities in Malaysia, the United Kingdom, Australia, the United States, Singapore, and beyond. The IGCSE is designed not just to test knowledge, but to reward higher-order thinking: analysis, evaluation, and application of concepts to unfamiliar situations.

## External Benchmarking

At the end of Year 8, students in Cambridge Lower Secondary sit the **Cambridge Lower Secondary Checkpoint** assessments in Mathematics, Science, and English. These externally marked assessments are benchmarked against thousands of Cambridge students worldwide, giving you and your child a clear, objective measure of their progress at the midpoint of their Secondary journey.

Checkpoint results help identify strengths to build on and areas that need additional focus — well in advance of the IGCSE examinations — so that your child enters Years 10 and 11 fully prepared and confident.
