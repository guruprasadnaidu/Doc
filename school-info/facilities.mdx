At Harvest International School, the learning environment is about far more than four walls and a whiteboard. Our campus has been thoughtfully designed to be comfortable, inspiring, and safe — a place where students genuinely enjoy coming to school and where teachers can do their best work every day.

## Our Campus

Located at Berkeley Complex in Klang, the Harvest campus provides modern facilities that support the school's philosophy of small-class, personalised education. Every space on campus has been arranged to encourage creativity, curiosity, and innovation. Whether your child is working through a Cambridge science experiment, collaborating on a group project, or finding a quiet corner to read, the campus provides the right environment for every kind of learning.

## Classroom Environment

Small class sizes are one of Harvest's defining features. By deliberately keeping classes small, we ensure that every student receives focused, individual attention from their teacher — rather than being lost in a crowd. Our classrooms are calm, well-organised, and nurturing spaces designed to minimise distraction and maximise engagement.

Technology is woven naturally into the classroom experience. Students use connective devices as part of their day-to-day learning, allowing teachers to deliver dynamic, interactive lessons and giving students hands-on experience with the digital tools they will use throughout their lives.

## Technology & Digital Learning

Harvest runs an integrated digital citizenship programme that goes beyond simply handing students a device. Students learn to engage with technology in ways that are:

- **Safe** — protecting personal information and staying secure online
- **Appropriate** — using digital tools in contexts that are relevant and constructive
- **On-task** — maintaining focus and avoiding digital distraction
- **Respectful** — communicating with others online with courtesy and care
- **Honest** — maintaining academic integrity in all digital work

ICT is also taught as a formal curriculum subject, ensuring students build structured technical knowledge alongside responsible digital habits.

## Sports & Recreational Facilities

Physical education and active recreation are an important part of life at Harvest. Our campus includes sports and games facilities that support the PE curriculum and provide a home for the popular Sports & Games Club. The school hosts an annual Sports Day, bringing the whole school community together in friendly competition. A range of co-curricular activities makes use of the recreational spaces throughout the school year, giving every student the chance to pursue their interests beyond the classroom.
