Harvest International School is committed to meeting internationally recognised standards of education. Our accreditations and affiliations reflect our dedication to academic quality, rigorous assessment, and preparing every student for success on the world stage.

## Ministry of Education Malaysia

Harvest International School is fully licensed by the Ministry of Education Malaysia (MoE) as a private international school. This licence ensures that Harvest meets all national standards and regulatory requirements governing private and international schools operating in Malaysia. Parents can be confident that their child is enrolled in a government-approved institution that upholds the highest standards of educational governance.

## Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE)

Harvest International School is an affiliated centre of Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE), the world's largest provider of international education programmes and qualifications. Through this affiliation, Harvest delivers the Cambridge Primary programme and the Cambridge Secondary curriculum, culminating in the globally respected Cambridge IGCSE.

CAIE is part of the University of Cambridge, one of the world's foremost academic institutions. As a not-for-profit organisation, CAIE is wholly focused on advancing high-quality education rather than commercial interests. Cambridge qualifications are recognised by leading universities, professional bodies, and employers in over 160 countries — giving Harvest graduates a genuine and lasting advantage when they apply for further study or enter the workforce.

## The Cambridge Pathway

The Cambridge Pathway provides a coherent, internationally benchmarked progression from early schooling through to pre-university study. At Harvest, students follow the pathway across four stages:

| Stage | Year Groups | Typical Ages |
|---|---|---|
| Cambridge Primary | Year 1 – Year 6 | Ages 7 – 11 |
| Cambridge Lower Secondary | Year 7 – Year 8 | Ages 12 – 13 |
| Cambridge Upper Secondary (IGCSE) | Year 9 – Year 11 | Ages 14 – 16 |
| Cambridge Advanced | Post-IGCSE | Ages 17 – 18 |

At the heart of every stage of the Cambridge education are five interconnected elements:

1. **International curriculum** — A broad, balanced, and flexible curriculum designed to challenge students and encourage independent thinking.
2. **Teaching and learning** — Pedagogical approaches that develop knowledge, understanding, and skills alongside positive learning habits.
3. **Assessment** — Rigorous, internationally benchmarked assessments that provide meaningful feedback and recognised qualifications.
4. **International recognition** — Qualifications accepted by top universities and employers worldwide.
5. **Global community** — Membership of a worldwide network of schools, teachers, and learners sharing best practices across borders.
