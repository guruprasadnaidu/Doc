## School Visits

We warmly encourage all prospective families to visit the Harvest campus before making their decision. There is no better way to get a feel for the school community, meet our teachers, and see the learning environment your child would be joining. Campus tours are available by appointment — simply email us at [enquiry@harvestintsch.edu.my](mailto:enquiry@harvestintsch.edu.my) or call **+60 3 3343 3633** to arrange a convenient time. We look forward to welcoming you.

## Admissions Office Hours

Our admissions team is available during school operating hours to answer your calls and respond to emails. For the most up-to-date information on office hours, please contact the school directly — we will be happy to confirm a time that works for you.

<Note>
  For the quickest response, email us at [enquiry@harvestintsch.edu.my](mailto:enquiry@harvestintsch.edu.my) and include your child's name, date of birth, and the year group you are enquiring about. This helps us give you accurate, personalised information straight away.
</Note>
