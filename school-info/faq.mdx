Choosing the right school for your child is one of the most important decisions you will make as a parent. To help you along the way, we have gathered answers to the questions we hear most often from families considering Harvest International School. If you do not find what you are looking for here, our admissions team is always happy to help.

## Admissions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What age groups does Harvest cater to?">
    Harvest International School welcomes students between the ages of 7 and 17. Our Cambridge Primary programme covers Year 1 through Year 6 (ages 7–12), and our Cambridge Secondary IGCSE pathway runs from Year 7 through Year 11 (ages 13–17).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you accept international students?">
    Yes — students of all nationalities are warmly welcome at Harvest. International students may be required to provide additional documentation, including a valid student pass and a copy of your child's passport. Please contact our admissions office directly for guidance on the specific requirements for your family's situation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I apply for enrolment?">
    You can begin the enrolment process by contacting our admissions team at [enquiry@harvestintsch.edu.my](mailto:enquiry@harvestintsch.edu.my) or by calling us on **+60 3 3343 3633**. For a full step-by-step guide, visit our [How to Apply](/admissions/how-to-apply) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there an entrance exam?">
    We may conduct a brief placement assessment before your child starts at Harvest. This is not a pass-or-fail exam — it simply helps us ensure that your child is placed in the year group that best matches their current level of knowledge and learning readiness, so they can settle in and thrive from day one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Programmes & Curriculum

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What curriculum does Harvest follow?">
    Harvest International School follows the Cambridge International curriculum, provided by Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE). We deliver the Cambridge Primary programme for younger students and the Cambridge Secondary curriculum leading to Cambridge IGCSE qualifications for older students.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What subjects are taught?">
    Our subject offering covers the full range of academic disciplines across both key stages:

    **Cambridge Primary (Year 1–6):** English, Mathematics, Malay, Science, ICT, Arts, and Physical Education.

    **Cambridge Secondary (Year 7–11):** English, Mathematics, Additional Mathematics, Malay, History, ICT, Business Studies, Economics, Biology, Chemistry, and Physics.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are class sizes large?">
    No. Harvest deliberately maintains small class sizes across all year groups. Keeping numbers low means your child receives personalised attention from their teacher, questions are answered promptly, and no student is overlooked. It is one of the features parents most consistently appreciate about studying at Harvest.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Harvest offer co-curricular activities?">
    Yes — life at Harvest extends well beyond the classroom. We offer a wide range of clubs and activities, including the Interact Club, St John Ambulance, Editorial Club, Speech & Drama, Chess Club, Christian Fellowship, and the Sports & Games Club. The school calendar also features annual events such as Sports Day, giving students plenty of opportunities to develop interests, build friendships, and grow as individuals.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
