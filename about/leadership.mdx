The character of any school is shaped, above all, by its people. At Harvest International School, our leadership team brings together a deep passion for education, decades of experience, and a shared conviction that every child deserves the very best start in life. Getting to know the people who lead our school will give you confidence in the community your child is joining.

## Founder — Rev. Alan Tan

Rev. Alan Tan founded Harvest International School with a clear and unwavering conviction: that true education must nurture the whole person. In his own words, a genuinely excellent school addresses not just the intellect, but the **moral, emotional, physical, psychological, and spiritual** dimensions of every student's life.

Rev. Tan's vision draws on a rich tradition of progressive educational thought. He looks to pioneers such as **Colonel Francis Parker**, who first championed child-centred learning in the nineteenth century, **John Dewey**, who argued that education is life itself and not merely preparation for it, **Maria Montessori**, who demonstrated that children flourish when given freedom within structure, and **Rudolf Steiner**, who integrated the arts, movement, and academic study into a unified vision of human development.

At the philosophical core of Rev. Tan's approach is Aristotle's framework of persuasion and character:

- **Ethos** — the credibility and moral character of the educator and the institution
- **Pathos** — the cultivation of emotional intelligence and genuine empathy in students
- **Logos** — the development of rigorous, disciplined, and independent reasoning

These three dimensions, working together, form the graduate that Harvest aspires to produce: a person of integrity, compassion, and intellect.

As Mahatma Gandhi once said:

> *"Education is not just literacy; it is the development of character."*

This is the founding principle that every teacher, every programme, and every school tradition at Harvest exists to honour.

## Principal — Dr Sheila Cheng

Dr Sheila Cheng warmly welcomes every family that considers joining the Harvest community. As Principal, she oversees the day-to-day life of the school and ensures that the Founder's vision is translated into a thriving, purposeful learning environment for every student.

Harvest International School — formerly known as **Harvest Academy** and established in **March 2012** — offers the internationally acclaimed **Cambridge curriculum** from **Primary Year 1 through to Secondary Year 11**, spanning students aged 7 to 17. Under Dr Cheng's leadership, the school continuously refines its teaching practice to ensure that the Cambridge IGCSE framework is delivered not as a set of boxes to tick, but as a living, dynamic educational experience.

Dr Cheng believes that a great school does three things above all else:

1. **Develops intellectual curiosity** — inspiring students to ask better questions, not just find correct answers.
2. **Nurtures creativity** — making space for imagination, original thinking, and artistic expression alongside analytical rigour.
3. **Builds character** — ensuring that students leave Harvest as people of principle who are ready to contribute positively to their communities and the wider world.

Her open-door approach means that parents and students are always encouraged to bring their questions, ideas, and concerns directly to the school's leadership.

## Board of Governors

Harvest International School is guided by a dedicated Board of Governors who provide strategic oversight, institutional accountability, and long-term direction for the school. The Board works in close partnership with the Founder and Principal to ensure that Harvest remains true to its founding values while continuing to grow and improve as an institution. Parents who wish to learn more about the governance structure of the school are welcome to contact the school office.
