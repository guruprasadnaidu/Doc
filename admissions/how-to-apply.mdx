Applying to Harvest International School is a straightforward process designed to be as clear and welcoming as possible for your family. From your first conversation with our admissions team to your child's first day in the classroom, we are here to guide you every step of the way. Follow the steps below to get started.

## Application Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make an Enquiry">
    Get in touch with our admissions team to begin. You can reach us by:

    - 📧 Email: [enquiry@harvestintsch.edu.my](mailto:enquiry@harvestintsch.edu.my)
    - 📞 Phone: +60 3 3343 3633 or +60 11 5895 4310

    Let us know your child's age, the year group you are enquiring about, and your preferred intake period. We will confirm current availability and provide you with all the information you need to move forward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule a School Visit">
    We invite you and your child to visit our campus in Klang. A guided tour gives your family the opportunity to see our classrooms and facilities in action, meet members of our teaching team, and get a genuine feel for the Harvest learning environment. Many families find that a visit confirms what they are looking for — a close-knit, purposeful school community where every student is known and supported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Application Form">
    Collect and submit your completed application form along with the required supporting documents. Ensure you include:

    - Your child's **birth certificate** (original or certified copy)
    - Recent **school reports** or academic records
    - A recent **passport-size photograph** (2 copies)
    - For international students: a copy of your child's **passport** and evidence of student visa status

    Secondary applicants should also include a **reference letter** from the previous school, if available. Our admissions team will let you know if any additional documents are needed for your child's specific circumstances.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sit the Placement Assessment">
    Once your application is received, we will schedule a brief placement assessment for your child. This is a relaxed and supportive exercise — not a competitive exam — designed to help our teachers understand your child's current level in core subjects and determine the most appropriate year group placement. We will advise you in advance on what to expect so your child feels comfortable and prepared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive an Offer">
    Following the assessment, our admissions team will review your application and placement results. If your child is offered a place, you will receive a formal **offer letter** confirming the year group, programme, and intake date. Read through the offer carefully, and do not hesitate to contact us with any questions before you confirm your acceptance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Enrolment">
    Accept your offer and complete the final enrolment steps:

    1. Pay the required enrolment and tuition fees (see [Fees & Finances](/admissions/fees-and-finances) for details)
    2. Return all signed enrolment paperwork to the admissions office
    3. Collect your child's **welcome pack**, which includes their timetable, uniform information, and everything they need for their first day

    Your child is now officially part of the Harvest community — welcome!
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Get a faster response by emailing us directly.** Send your enquiry to [enquiry@harvestintsch.edu.my](mailto:enquiry@harvestintsch.edu.my) and include your child's **full name**, **date of birth**, and **intended year group**. This allows us to check availability and respond with the most relevant information right away.
</Note>
