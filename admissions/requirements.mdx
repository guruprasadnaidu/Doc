Before you begin your application, it helps to know exactly what to prepare. Harvest International School keeps the admissions process straightforward so that you can focus on what matters most — finding the right environment for your child. Below you will find the core eligibility criteria and required documents for each programme, along with guidance for international applicants.

## Admission Criteria by Programme

Review the requirements for the programme that matches your child's age and previous schooling.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cambridge Primary">
    **Age Range:** 7–12 years old (Year 1–6)

    Cambridge Primary is the ideal starting point for younger learners who are ready to build strong foundations in English, Mathematics, and Science within a structured international framework.

    **Academic Eligibility**

    - Your child should fall within the **7–12 age range** at the point of enrolment
    - A basic level of **English proficiency** is expected, as all lessons are delivered in English
    - Previous formal schooling is advantageous but not always mandatory — contact admissions if your child is transitioning from home schooling or a non-English medium school

    **Required Documents**

    - Original or certified copy of your child's **birth certificate**
    - Recent **passport-size photograph** (2 copies)
    - **Previous school reports** or academic records (most recent year)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cambridge Secondary">
    **Age Range:** 13–17 years old (Year 7–11)

    Our Cambridge Secondary programme leads students through to the internationally recognised IGCSE qualifications. Students joining at this level are expected to have a solid academic foundation, ideally within the Cambridge framework.

    **Academic Eligibility**

    - Your child should fall within the **13–17 age range** at the point of enrolment
    - Completion of **Cambridge Primary** or an equivalent primary education programme is required
    - Strong **English proficiency** is expected across reading, writing, and verbal communication

    **Required Documents**

    - Original or certified copy of your child's **birth certificate**
    - Recent **passport-size photograph** (2 copies)
    - **Previous school reports** or academic records (minimum last two years)
    - **Reference letter** from the previous school (recommended, if available)
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## International Students

Harvest International School warmly welcomes students of all nationalities. Our diverse community enriches the learning experience for every student, and our team is experienced in supporting families relocating to Malaysia.

If your child is not a Malaysian citizen, you will likely need to provide the following additional documentation:

- A valid **student visa** (or evidence that one is being processed)
- A copy of your child's **passport** (all relevant pages)

We recommend contacting our admissions team early so we can advise you on the specific visa and documentation requirements applicable to your family's situation.

## Placement Assessment

To ensure your child is placed in the year group where they will thrive, we may conduct a brief **placement assessment** prior to enrolment. This is not a competitive entrance exam — it is a short, supportive exercise designed to help our teachers understand your child's current level in core subjects and identify the best starting point for their learning journey.

The admissions team will advise you on the format and timing of the assessment once your application is under review.
