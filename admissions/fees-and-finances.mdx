At Harvest International School, our fees are structured to reflect the quality of the Cambridge education, the expertise of our teaching team, and the facilities and resources we provide to every student. We are committed to being transparent about costs so that your family can plan with confidence. Please read this page for an overview of what fees cover and how payments are managed — and reach out to our admissions team for the most up-to-date figures.

<Warning>
  **Fee information is subject to annual review.** For current and accurate fee amounts, please contact our admissions office directly at [enquiry@harvestintsch.edu.my](mailto:enquiry@harvestintsch.edu.my) or call +60 3 3343 3633. The figures published in any third-party source may not reflect the most recent schedule.

## What Fees Cover

Your child's tuition fees contribute to a comprehensive educational experience. Fees at Harvest International School typically cover:

- **Cambridge curriculum tuition** across all core and elective subjects, delivered by qualified and experienced teachers
- **Access to co-curricular programmes**, including enrichment activities designed to develop the whole child beyond the academic syllabus
- **Use of school facilities**, including classrooms, learning spaces, and any shared student resources on campus
- **Learning materials and resources** required for your child's coursework and day-to-day lessons

Our admissions team can provide a detailed breakdown of what is included in each programme's fee structure when you make an enquiry.

## Payment Schedule

Fees are typically structured and paid on a **per-term basis**, in line with the academic calendar. This allows families to plan payments around each term's start date rather than managing a single large annual sum. The exact schedule — including the number of terms, due dates, and accepted payment methods — is provided during the admissions process.

Contact our admissions office to receive the current academic calendar and fee payment schedule relevant to your child's programme and intended intake date.

## Financial Enquiries

We encourage you to raise any questions about fees openly during the admissions process. Our team is happy to walk you through the full cost of enrolment, answer questions about what is and is not included, and help you understand the payment process before you commit.
