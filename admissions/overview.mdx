Welcome to Harvest International School — a Cambridge-affiliated international school in Klang, Malaysia, dedicated to delivering world-class education to students aged 7 to 17. Since our establishment in 2013, we have been committed to nurturing confident, well-rounded learners through the internationally recognised Cambridge curriculum, supported by a caring team and a close-knit school community. Whether your child is joining us for Cambridge Primary or stepping into the Cambridge Secondary IGCSE pathway, you will find a warm, purposeful environment where every student is known by name.

## Why Choose Harvest?

Harvest International School is more than a place of learning — it is a community that invests in every student's potential. Here is what sets us apart:

- **Small class sizes** that allow teachers to give each student the individual attention they deserve
- **Personalised learning** tailored to your child's pace, strengths, and areas for growth
- **Cambridge curriculum** recognised by universities and schools in over 160 countries
- **Holistic development** through co-curricular activities, character education, and values-based learning
- **Ministry of Education Malaysia licensed**, giving you confidence in our standards and accountability
- **Established since 2013**, with a proven track record of guiding students through Cambridge milestones

## Intake & Enrolment

We accept applications on a **rolling basis** throughout the year, subject to available places in each year group. Because class sizes are intentionally kept small, spaces can fill quickly — especially at the start of a new academic term. We encourage you to reach out to our admissions team as early as possible to check current availability for your child's year group and to begin the application process.
