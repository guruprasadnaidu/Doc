## Your Journey to Harvest

Joining our school community is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to get started.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Explore Programmes">
    Review our Cambridge Primary and Cambridge Secondary offerings to find the right fit for your child's age, learning style, and aspirations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Admissions Requirements">
    Visit the Admissions Overview to confirm eligibility criteria, required documents, and key intake dates for the upcoming academic year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Your Application">
    Complete and submit your application form along with the required supporting documents. Our admissions team will be in touch to guide you through the next steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Welcome to Harvest">
    Once your place is confirmed, you will receive a full orientation pack. Join our community and begin a Cambridge education journey built on wisdom, courage, and character.
  </Step>
</Steps>
