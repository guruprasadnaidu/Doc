At Harvest International School, student development extends far beyond the walls of the classroom. Alongside a rigorous Cambridge curriculum, you will find a vibrant co-curricular programme that encourages every student to discover new passions, build meaningful friendships, and grow into a well-rounded individual. Whether your child is drawn to the arts, athletics, community service, or academic competitions, there is a place for them to thrive at Harvest.

## Why Co-Curricular Matters

Harvest believes that truly holistic education means nurturing the whole child — academically, socially, physically, and creatively. Academic excellence is only one dimension of a flourishing young person. Through clubs, events, and sporting activities, students develop the character, confidence, and interpersonal skills that prepare them for success in university, careers, and life. In Harvest's small-class, nurturing environment, every student has the opportunity — and the encouragement — to get involved, take on leadership roles, and make their mark on the school community.

## Benefits of Co-Curricular Involvement

Taking part in co-curricular activities gives your child advantages that go well beyond the school timetable. Students who engage actively in life outside the classroom consistently demonstrate:

- **Leadership development** — taking initiative, guiding peers, and building decision-making confidence
- **Teamwork and collaboration** — learning to work constructively with others toward shared goals
- **Creativity and self-expression** — finding their voice through drama, writing, music, and the arts
- **Physical health and wellbeing** — staying active, building healthy habits, and enjoying competitive sport
- **Social skills and friendships** — forming lasting bonds across year groups and backgrounds
