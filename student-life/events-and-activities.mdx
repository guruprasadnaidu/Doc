Harvest's school calendar is filled with events that bring the entire community together — students, teachers, and families alike. From high-energy sporting competitions to cultural celebrations and creative showcases, these occasions are more than just highlights on the timetable. They are opportunities for students to grow in confidence, deepen their sense of belonging, and create memories that last far beyond their school years.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sports Day" icon="flag-checkered">
    Sports Day is one of the most anticipated events of the school year. Students compete across a range of athletic events as part of their respective houses, bringing healthy rivalry, house spirit, and genuine teamwork to the forefront. It is a celebration of physical achievement and the collective pride that comes from competing together.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Harvest's Got Talent" icon="star">
    Harvest's Got Talent is the school's annual talent showcase, where students take the stage to perform music, dance, comedy, spoken word, and more. This much-loved event celebrates the extraordinary creativity and confidence of the student body, giving every performer a moment to shine in front of the whole school community.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Field Trips" icon="map-location-dot">
    Educational field trips take students beyond the classroom and into the wider world. Visits to museums, cultural heritage sites, nature reserves, and other locations bring curriculum topics to life and give students real-world knowledge and experiences that textbooks alone cannot provide.
  </Card>
  <Card title="English Month" icon="book-open">
    English Month is a dedicated, month-long celebration of the English language and all that it enables. Through competitions, performances, creative activities, and collaborative projects, students deepen their appreciation for English as a tool for communication, literature, and self-expression.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Merdeka Celebration" icon="flag">
    The Merdeka Celebration marks Malaysia's National Day and is an important occasion in the Harvest calendar. Students learn about the nation's history, heritage, and values, fostering a sense of national pride and cultural awareness that connects them to the broader Malaysian story.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chinese New Year" icon="calendar">
    The Chinese New Year celebration is a vibrant and joyful event that brings the whole school community together. Marking one of the most significant festivals in the Malaysian cultural calendar, it is an opportunity for students to share, learn, and celebrate the richness of cultural diversity within the Harvest family.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Chapel Service

Regular Chapel Services form an important part of Harvest's spiritual and values-based community life. These gatherings provide students with a space for reflection, shared values, and a sense of community that goes beyond the academic. Chapel Services reflect Harvest's commitment to nurturing not just the minds, but also the hearts and character of its students.
