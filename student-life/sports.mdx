Physical activity and sport are central to Harvest International School's holistic education philosophy. Harvest recognises that a healthy body supports a healthy mind, and that the lessons learned through sport — resilience, teamwork, fair play, and self-discipline — are among the most valuable a young person can carry into adult life. Whether your child is a committed competitor or simply enjoys staying active, Harvest provides the environment and opportunities for every student to engage with sport meaningfully.

## Physical Education

Physical Education is a core part of the curriculum at all year levels at Harvest. Through structured PE lessons, students develop physical literacy — the movement skills, knowledge, and confidence they need to stay active for life. Classes focus on coordination, fitness, and healthy habits, ensuring that every student builds a strong physical foundation alongside their academic studies. PE is not just about performance; it is about helping students understand the importance of looking after their bodies and enjoying the process of being active.

## Sports & Games Club

For students who want to take their enthusiasm for sport further, the Sports & Games Club offers a dedicated space to pursue physical activity beyond regular PE classes. The club covers a range of competitive and recreational sports and games, giving students the flexibility to explore different disciplines and find the ones they love most. Through the club, students develop teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship in a supportive, inclusive setting — values that extend well beyond the playing field.

## Sports Day

<Card title="Annual Sports Day" icon="flag-checkered">
  Sports Day is one of the highlights of the Harvest school year. Students compete as members of their respective houses across a range of athletic events, bringing energy, determination, and house pride to every race and competition. It is a day that celebrates physical achievement and the spirit of friendly rivalry — a powerful reminder of what students can accomplish when they train hard, compete fairly, and support one another. Sports Day is a true community occasion, with the whole school coming together to cheer, compete, and celebrate.
</Card>

## Sports Values

Sport at Harvest is guided by a set of core values that shape how students engage with physical activity and competition. These values are reinforced across PE classes, the Sports & Games Club, and Sports Day alike:

- **Teamwork** — achieving more together than any individual could alone
- **Resilience** — learning to push through challenges and bounce back from setbacks
- **Fair play** — competing with integrity and respecting opponents, teammates, and officials
- **Physical wellbeing** — prioritising health, fitness, and active living as lifelong habits
- **Community and house spirit** — building pride, belonging, and camaraderie within the school
