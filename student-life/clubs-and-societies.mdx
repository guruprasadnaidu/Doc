---

Harvest International School offers a wide range of clubs and societies, giving every student the chance to explore their interests, develop new skills, and build friendships beyond the classroom. Whether your child is passionate about community service, the written word, strategic games, or the performing arts, you will find a club at Harvest where they belong. These activities are an integral part of the enriched student life that Harvest is proud to provide.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Interact Club" icon="handshake">
    The Interact Club is Rotary International's official youth programme, bringing together students who are committed to community service and developing leadership skills. Members organise and participate in service projects that make a real difference locally, while growing their sense of social responsibility and their ability to lead with purpose.
  </Card>
  <Card title="St John Ambulance" icon="kit-medical">
    The St John Ambulance unit provides students with comprehensive first aid training and health and safety awareness. Members work toward recognised certifications in life-saving skills, gaining practical knowledge and the confidence to respond effectively in emergency situations — capabilities that serve them throughout their lives.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Editorial Club" icon="pen-nib">
    The Editorial Club is the home of Harvest's young writers, journalists, and storytellers. Students contribute to school publications, practise the craft of writing, and develop strong communication skills across a variety of formats — from news articles and features to creative writing and editorial commentary.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Speech and Drama" icon="microphone">
    Speech and Drama gives students a platform to develop their voice, build confidence, and explore the world of performance. Through public speaking exercises, scripted performances, and improvisation, students refine their stage presence, overcome nervousness, and discover the power of expressive communication.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chess Club" icon="chess">
    The Chess Club challenges students to think critically, plan strategically, and solve complex problems — all while having fun. Members develop concentration and analytical thinking through regular play, and have the opportunity to test their skills in both internal school competitions and external tournaments.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Christian Fellowship" icon="cross">
    The Christian Fellowship is a warm, welcoming community group for students of faith. It provides a space for spiritual support, meaningful fellowship, and values-based discussions that encourage personal reflection and a sense of belonging within the broader Harvest community.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sports & Games Club" icon="futbol">
    The Sports & Games Club is the go-to destination for students who want to pursue physical activity beyond regular PE lessons. Covering a range of competitive and recreational sports and games, the club fosters physical fitness, teamwork, and a healthy spirit of sportsmanship in a fun and inclusive environment.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Club availability may vary by term depending on enrolment and scheduling. Contact the school directly for the current list of active clubs and to find out how your child can sign up.
</Note>
